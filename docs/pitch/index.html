0:40 – 1:10 · how we do it (30 s)
  • NASA data is the core: NASADEM, 5 MODIS products, VIIRS, MERRA-2 on Earth; LRO LOLA (computed by us) plus Diviner, LEND, LROC, LCROSS values on the Moon. In the app every value names its sensor.
  • Key point: slopes, roughness and craters computed by us on LOLA with the same definition as on Earth (30 m). Filled chips = computed by us; outlined = published mission values.
  • If asked: per parameter, similarity = exp(−(D/τ)²) with D the distance in Earth percentiles; weighted per test.
  • Map: the demo case. Orange = places nobody tests yet for Aristarchus × rover; white = the best ones, already used; rings = 1,500 and 3,000 km from Turin.
  • Click: validation. Sites where agencies and companies really test come out on top without telling the model: SP Crater (NASA training) is the #1 hexagon for Haworth × rover; Mojave, Etna, Mauna Kea in the top 4–11%.
  • Click: what Earth can never give (challenge: “remember Earth’s limits”).

“We did not tell the model where companies test. It found those places by itself, and then found better ones.”
