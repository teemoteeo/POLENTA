And nobody measures how faithful a test site is to the real target. Curiosity’s wheels were tested on smoother ground than Mars, and got damaged early.

0:15 – 0:50 · problem (35 s)
  • Real case, public documents: survey Sep 2025 → plan Feb 2026 → 4–5 months of permits → campaign Jul 2026.
  • Click: three numbers. 10 months; 57 person-days (our estimate); crater fields for landing sensors are built by hand (Morpheus ~$14M).
  • Click: the real pain is fidelity. If the test ground is easier than the target, the test lies.

“Ten months to choose a test site, by hand. And nobody can tell you how close it really is to the Moon.”
